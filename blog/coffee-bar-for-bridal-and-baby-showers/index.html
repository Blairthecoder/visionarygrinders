How to Plan a Coffee Bar for a Bridal or Baby Shower

Published September 30, 2026 by Visionary Grinders

Quick Answer

Keep the menu short, pick one signature drink, and place the coffee bar where guests can gather without blocking the food. A coffee pop-up handles the making so the host doesn't have to.

Pick a Short, Friendly Menu

Three to five options is plenty for a shower. Include a signature latte and at least one iced choice. Our menu includes honey vanilla and brown sugar lattes that suit a wide range of tastes.

Choose a Signature Drink

A signature drink gives guests something to talk about and gives you a photo moment. Ask about flavors that fit your theme or colors.

Place the Bar Thoughtfully

Put the coffee bar away from the main food table so lines don't collide. Leave room around it so guests can wait and chat.

Time It to the Event

Brunch showers tend to use more coffee early. Afternoon showers may lean toward iced drinks. Share your timing in the inquiry so we can plan for it.

Let Someone Else Handle the Pouring

A pop-up means the host and family can enjoy the party while a host serves the drinks. Start on our private celebrations page or send an inquiry.
