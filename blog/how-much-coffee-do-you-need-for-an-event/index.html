How Much Coffee Do You Need for an Event?

Published September 30, 2026 by Visionary Grinders

Quick Answer

A common planning range is one to two drinks per guest for a two- to three-hour event, and more for morning events. Guest count, timing and whether drinks are made to order all change the number.

Quick Coffee Calculator

Uses the common planning range of one to two drinks per guest for a two- to three-hour event. It is a starting point, not a quote.

Start With Guest Count and Event Length

Guest count is the starting point, and event length is the multiplier. A short afternoon party needs fewer drinks per person than a full morning of meetings.

Account for Time of Day

Morning events tend to have higher coffee demand. Evening events may see more guests choosing one drink and stopping. If your event is early, plan toward the higher end of the range.

Think About Hot vs. Iced

In Houston's warm climate, iced drinks often move faster. Offering both hot and iced options gives guests a choice. See our guide to hot vs. iced coffee for Houston events.

Remember Service Speed

Made-to-order drinks take a little longer than batch coffee, but guests get exactly what they want. A focused menu keeps the line moving.
