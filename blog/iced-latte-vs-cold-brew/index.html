Iced Latte vs. Cold Brew: Which Should You Serve?

Published September 30, 2026 by Visionary Grinders

Quick Answer

An iced latte is espresso with milk over ice. Cold brew is coffee steeped in cold water, then served over ice. Most events do well with both.

What Is an Iced Latte?

An iced latte combines espresso, milk and ice, and it can be flavored. It's a good option for guests who like a creamier, sweeter drink.

What Is Cold Brew?

Cold brew is made by steeping coffee grounds in cold water, which produces a smooth, less acidic drink. It's a natural fit for guests who prefer black coffee.

Which One Fits Your Event?

  • Crowds and outdoor events: cold brew is simple and refreshing.
  • Celebrations: flavored iced lattes are a hit.
  • Mixed groups: offer both.
