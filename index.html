Meet the Founder

Coffee, Hospitality and Conversation

Visionary Grinders was started by Jasmine Rhodes, a Houston sports performance coach who spends her days helping people grow and believe in what's possible. The coffee bar is another way to do that: create a space to pause, connect and leave encouraged.

How Booking Works

Three Simple Steps to a Coffee Pop-Up

  1. Send Your Details. Share your date, guest count, location and event type through the inquiry form.
  2. Talk It Through. We follow up to discuss your event and what we can serve.
  3. Enjoy the Coffee. We show up, set up and serve your guests.
