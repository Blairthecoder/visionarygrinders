Book a Houston Coffee Pop-Up

Tell us about your event. Share a few details and we'll follow up to talk through your event and what we can serve.

What Happens Next

  1. 1. You send the details. Date, guest count, location, event type and what you have in mind.
  2. 2. We follow up. We talk through your event, the setup and what we can serve.
  3. 3. We confirm. Once the plan fits, we lock in your date.

Booking Questions

What Should I Include in My Inquiry?

Your event date, estimated guest count, venue or city, event type and any drinks or timing you have in mind. The form asks for exactly that.

How Far in Advance Should I Book?

Send your inquiry as soon as you have a date. Popular dates, including graduation season and holidays, fill sooner.

What Does a Coffee Pop-Up Cost?

Pricing depends on guest count, event length, menu, location and setup. We quote each event after we have your details. See what affects the cost of a coffee pop-up.

Where Do You Serve?

Houston, Katy, Sugar Land, The Woodlands, Pearland, Cypress, Spring, Missouri City and Humble. If you are nearby but not listed, send your location.
