Celebration Planning Guide

How to Plan Coffee for a Private Celebration

The best celebration coffee bars feel like part of the party, not a utility. Guests of every age drift toward them, so placement matters: put the bar where people already gather, with room for a short line and a place to stand with a drink.

Think about the mix of guests. A birthday or shower often includes kids, grandparents and friends who do not drink alcohol. A short menu with a sweet latte, an iced option and a stronger espresso drink covers that range without overwhelming the line.

A Menu That Fits the Day

  • Signature lattes, including honey vanilla and brown sugar, for a crowd-pleasing sweet option
  • Iced lattes for warm afternoons and outdoor parties
  • A classic espresso drink for guests who want something stronger
  • Cold brew as a smooth, easy-to-serve option

Common Planning Mistakes to Avoid

  • Booking late and finding your date is taken. Send your inquiry as soon as the date is set.
  • Hiding the coffee bar in a corner. Put it where guests already mingle.
  • Planning only hot drinks for a warm Houston afternoon. Offer iced lattes or cold brew.
  • Forgetting to mention venue rules, power and access when you inquire.
