Workplace Planning Guide

How to Plan a Coffee Pop-Up for Your Office

A workplace coffee pop-up works best when it is announced ahead of time and tied to a moment: a kickoff, a client visit, an appreciation morning. People should know when it is happening and where.

Plan around how your team actually moves. Offices rarely fill in an even stream. Expect a rush at the start, a lull, and a second wave before meetings. Share your schedule so service can be planned around those waves.

How Booking Works

  1. Send your date, estimated guest count, location and event type through the inquiry form.
  2. We follow up to talk through your workplace event and what we can serve.
  3. Together we settle the setup, timing and access details.
  4. We arrive, set up and serve guests made-to-order drinks.

A Menu That Fits the Day

  • Espresso drinks and cold brew to keep service moving for a larger group
  • Specialty lattes for people who want a treat
  • Hot and iced options so the menu works at any time of year
  • A short menu that moves faster than a long list

Common Planning Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not sharing building access, parking and loading details, a detail that often gets missed.
  • Choosing a side room nobody walks past. Pick a lobby, kitchen entrance or courtyard.
  • Skipping the announcement. A short note the day before helps people plan to attend.
  • Expecting an even flow. Plan for waves around meetings and breaks.
