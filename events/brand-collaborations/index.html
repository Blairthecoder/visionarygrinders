Brand Activation Guide

How to Plan a Coffee Activation for Your Brand

A coffee bar is one of the easiest ways to give a launch or pop-up shop a reason for people to stop. It photographs well, gives guests something to hold, and creates a natural moment to talk about what you are launching.

Decide what you want from the activation before you inquire: foot traffic, social content, or relationship-building with a select guest list. That goal shapes the setup, the menu and the hours.

How Booking Works

  1. Send your date, estimated guest count, location and event type through the inquiry form.
  2. We follow up to talk through your brand event and what we can serve.
  3. Together we settle the setup, timing and access details.
  4. We arrive, set up and serve guests made-to-order drinks.

A Menu That Fits the Day

  • A focused drink list that fits your launch or activation
  • Signature lattes as a photo-friendly centerpiece
  • Iced options for outdoor and high-traffic events
  • Cold brew for quick service at busy moments

Common Planning Mistakes to Avoid

  • Booking without a clear goal. Know whether you want traffic, content or conversations.
  • Leaving brand details for the last week. Share colors and any branding needs early.
  • Underestimating line time at launch peaks. Share expected turnout in your inquiry.
  • Skipping a plan for guest flow between the coffee bar and your product.
