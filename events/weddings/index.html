Beyond the Drink Table

Where a Coffee Bar Fits in a Wedding Weekend

A coffee bar works at more than one moment: a welcome brunch, a rehearsal dinner, the cocktail hour for guests who want something else, or a late-night send-off.

It is also a good choice for couples who want an option other than alcohol that still feels like part of the celebration.

  • Welcome brunches and morning-after farewells
  • Rehearsal dinners and engagement parties
  • Reception coffee bars and dessert pairings
  • Bridal showers and bachelorette brunches

Wedding Planning Guide

How to Plan a Coffee Bar for Your Wedding Weekend

The best wedding coffee bars are tied to a specific moment: a welcome brunch, a cocktail hour alternative, a late-night send-off. Choosing the moment first makes everything else easier, including the hours and the menu.

Check your venue rules early. Some venues restrict outside vendors, power or setup times. Send those details with your inquiry so the plan works for both your venue and your timeline.

How Booking Works

  1. Send your date, estimated guest count, location and event type through the inquiry form.
  2. We follow up to talk through your wedding and what we can serve.
  3. Together we settle the setup, timing and access details.
  4. We arrive, set up and serve guests made-to-order drinks.

A Menu That Fits the Day

  • Signature lattes, including honey vanilla and brown sugar, for a sweet crowd favorite
  • Iced lattes and cold brew for warm Houston weddings
  • A classic espresso drink for late-night energy
  • Hot lattes for brunches and cooler months

Common Planning Mistakes to Avoid

  • Booking the coffee bar before checking venue vendor rules.
  • Choosing a spot that blocks the guest flow between ceremony and reception.
  • Treating it as an afterthought. Decide the moment and hours up front.
  • Forgetting guests who want an iced option in warm weather.
