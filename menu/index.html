Coffee Pop-Up Menu: Specialty Lattes, Espresso and Cold Brew

Every drink is made to order at your event in Houston, so guests get a fresh cup and a real conversation.

Signature Drinks

Specialty Lattes

Our lattes are the crowd favorite. Signature flavors include honey vanilla and brown sugar, served hot or iced, with a layered look that photographs well at any event.

Looking for something specific for a shower, a team morning or a launch? Tell us in your inquiry and we'll talk through the options.

Espresso Bar Classics

Espresso Drinks

Classic espresso-bar favorites, pulled fresh at your event. A good option for workplace events and morning gatherings where guests want a familiar order done well.

Made for a Crowd

Cold Brew

Smooth, slow-steeped cold brew that keeps up with a busy line. It's a natural fit for outdoor events, markets and community gatherings across Houston.

Pair it with a latte menu so guests have both a bold black option and something sweeter.
