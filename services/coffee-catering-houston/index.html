How Much Coffee Does Your Group Need?

A gallon of coffee pours about 16 eight-ounce cups. For a two-hour event, plan on roughly one and a half to two drinks per guest, and a little more when the weather is cold or the event runs through a morning.

You do not have to work it out yourself. Send your guest count and we will size the service, the menu and the number of baristas to match. Our guide on how much coffee you need for an event walks through the math.

Common Catering Mistakes to Avoid

  • Comparing a drop-off quote with a staffed bar as if they were the same service.
  • Not asking whether drinks are made to order or brewed in advance.
  • Forgetting to check power, parking, load-in and vendor rules at the venue.
  • Underestimating the guest count, so the line outlasts the event.
  • Booking late on a popular weekend or in graduation season.

Frequently Asked Questions

What Is Catered Coffee?

Catered coffee is coffee service brought to your event instead of guests going out for it. It ranges from drop-off urns to a staffed coffee bar. Visionary Grinders caters the staffed kind: a barista sets up at your venue in Houston and makes each drink to order.

How Much Does Coffee Catering Cost in Houston?

It depends on guest count, how long you want service, the menu and the venue. We quote each event after you send your date, guest count and location through the inquiry form.

How Many Cups of Coffee Are in a Gallon?

A gallon of coffee pours about 16 eight-ounce cups. For a two-hour event, plan on roughly one and a half to two drinks per guest.

Do You Cater Coffee for Weddings and Showers?

Yes. Coffee bars work well at wedding receptions, bridal showers, baby showers, graduations and birthdays, either during the reception or as a late-afternoon pick-me-up.

Do You Offer Office and Corporate Coffee Catering?

Yes. We cater team mornings, client days, employee appreciation events and conferences across Houston. Share your headcount and schedule in the inquiry.

Is Coffee Catering the Same as a Mobile Coffee Bar?

Not always. Coffee catering can mean drop-off coffee, while a mobile coffee bar serves drinks made to order. We do the second, and our guide explains the difference.

Do You Drop Off Coffee Boxes or Urns?

Our service is a staffed bar with drinks made in front of your guests, not a drop-off. Tell us what you need in the inquiry and we will follow up.
