Mobile Coffee Bar Guide

What to Know Before Booking a Mobile Coffee Bar

A mobile coffee bar is a great fit when you want coffee to be part of the experience. It comes to your venue, serves drinks made to order, and gives guests a host and a conversation along with the cup.

Before you book, know your date, an estimated guest count, your venue and the hours you want service. Those four details are the foundation of a good plan.

How Booking Works

  1. Send your date, estimated guest count, location and event type through the inquiry form.
  2. We follow up to talk through your event and what we can serve.
  3. Together we settle the setup, timing and access details.
  4. We arrive, set up and serve guests made-to-order drinks.

A Menu That Fits the Day

  • Specialty lattes, including honey vanilla and brown sugar
  • Classic espresso drinks
  • Cold brew, ready for a crowd
  • Hot or iced, made to order

Common Planning Mistakes to Avoid

  • Booking without checking venue rules for outside vendors.
  • Not planning for outdoor shade or weather.
  • Leaving guest count vague.
  • Waiting to inquire until the last few weeks on a popular date.

What Is a Mobile Coffee Bar?
