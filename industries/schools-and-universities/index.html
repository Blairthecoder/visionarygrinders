Campus Planning Guide

How to Plan Coffee for a School or Campus Event

School and campus events run on tight schedules. Share your event hours, break times and expected headcount, so the coffee bar can be planned around when people actually have time.

For PTA and booster club fundraisers, say so in your inquiry. A coffee bar can anchor the event, and we can talk through how it fits your goals.

How Booking Works

  1. Send your date, estimated guest count, location and event type through the inquiry form.
  2. We follow up to talk through your school event and what we can serve.
  3. Together we settle the setup, timing and access details.
  4. We arrive, set up and serve guests made-to-order drinks.

A Menu That Fits the Day

  • Cold brew and iced lattes for outdoor events
  • Hot lattes and espresso drinks for cooler mornings
  • Specialty lattes for appreciation events
  • A short menu that keeps lines moving between periods

Common Planning Mistakes to Avoid

  • Ignoring class and break schedules when picking service hours.
  • Forgetting campus access, parking and visitor check-in.
  • Not telling your inquiry that it is a fundraiser.
  • Planning only hot drinks for an outdoor Houston event.
