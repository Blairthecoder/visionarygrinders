Launch Planning Guide

How to Plan Coffee for a Retail Launch or Grand Opening

A grand opening needs a reason to stop in and a reason to stay. A coffee bar does both, and gives your launch a natural photo moment for social content.

Decide your goal first: traffic, content or relationships with a guest list. That shapes the hours, the setup and the menu.

How Booking Works

  1. Send your date, estimated guest count, location and event type through the inquiry form.
  2. We follow up to talk through your launch and what we can serve.
  3. Together we settle the setup, timing and access details.
  4. We arrive, set up and serve guests made-to-order drinks.

A Menu That Fits the Day

  • Signature lattes as a photo-friendly centerpiece
  • Iced lattes and cold brew for outdoor and busy launches
  • Espresso drinks for quick service
  • A short branded menu that fits your concept

Common Planning Mistakes to Avoid

  • Opening without a plan for peak-hour lines.
  • Hiding the coffee bar from the storefront.
  • Skipping brand details until the last week.
  • Not promoting the coffee bar in launch announcements.
