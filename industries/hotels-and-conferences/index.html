A coffee bar attendees remember between sessions.

Quick Answer

Event Hospitality

How a Coffee Pop-Up Helps an Event

Conferences run on breaks. A coffee bar turns a break into a place where attendees meet, and gives sponsors and organizers a branded moment.

It suits hotel lobbies, expo floors, welcome mornings and VIP lounges.

  • Conference welcome and registration mornings
  • Expo floor and sponsor activations
  • Hotel guest and lobby events
  • Corporate retreats and offsites

Conference Planning Guide

How to Plan Coffee for a Hotel or Conference Event

Conference coffee works when it is where attendees are between sessions. Share your agenda and break times so the bar can be ready for each wave.

Load-in and access matter at hotels and convention venues. Send the venue, loading information and any vendor rules with your inquiry.

How Booking Works

  1. Send your date, estimated guest count, location and event type through the inquiry form.
  2. We follow up to talk through your conference and what we can serve.
  3. Together we settle the setup, timing and access details.
  4. We arrive, set up and serve guests made-to-order drinks.

A Menu That Fits the Day

  • Espresso drinks and cold brew for fast service between sessions
  • Specialty lattes for VIP lounges and welcome mornings
  • Hot and iced options for any season
  • A short menu for fast lines during breaks

Common Planning Mistakes to Avoid

  • Not sharing break times, so service misses the rush.
  • Forgetting loading and vendor rules at the venue.
  • Placing the bar far from session rooms.
  • Underestimating attendance at peak breaks.
