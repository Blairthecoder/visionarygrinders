Office Coffee, Elevated

Why Offices Book a Coffee Pop-Up

A coffee pop-up gives a routine day a focal point. Instead of a pot of batch coffee in the break room, your team gets a real latte and a reason to step away from the desk.

Office managers and event planners use it for quarterly kickoffs, client visits and appreciation days, because it makes the day feel planned without adding a catering project.

  • Team kickoffs and all-hands mornings
  • Client appreciation and open house days
  • Employee appreciation and milestone celebrations
  • Lobby, courtyard or conference-floor setups

Office Planning Guide

How to Plan Coffee for a Corporate or Office Event

Corporate events work best when coffee is tied to a moment people care about: a kickoff, a client visit, a milestone. That gives the pop-up a reason to exist and gives attendees a reason to come.

Share your headcount, the hours you want coffee, and building access details. Office managers who send access, parking and loading information up front avoid the most common day-of problems.

How Booking Works

  1. Send your date, estimated guest count, location and event type through the inquiry form.
  2. We follow up to talk through your office event and what we can serve.
  3. Together we settle the setup, timing and access details.
  4. We arrive, set up and serve guests made-to-order drinks.

A Menu That Fits the Day

  • Espresso drinks and cold brew for larger teams
  • Specialty lattes for appreciation days
  • Hot and iced options for any season
  • A short menu that keeps lines moving

Common Planning Mistakes to Avoid

  • Leaving building access and parking details until the day of.
  • Setting up in a room off the main traffic path.
  • Announcing it the morning of instead of the day before.
  • Planning for an even flow instead of waves around meetings.
