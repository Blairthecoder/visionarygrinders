About Visionary Grinders and Founder Jasmine Rhodes

A Houston coffee pop-up built on hospitality, conversation and forward momentum.

Our Story

Hi, I'm Jasmine

I'm the founder of Visionary Grinders and a sports performance coach here in Houston. A lot of my life revolves around helping people grow, pursue their goals, and believe in what's possible. Visionary Grinders is another expression of that passion, through coffee, hospitality, and conversation.

I'm building this one experience at a time, from private celebrations to community gatherings and pop-ups. I want to create space for people to pause, connect, and leave encouraged, and for a cup of our coffee to be a small, meaningful part of someone's day.

Jasmine Rhodes, Founder
