Coffee Pop-Up FAQs: Booking, Drinks and Events

Quick answers about booking Visionary Grinders for your Houston event.

What Is Visionary Grinders?

Visionary Grinders is a Houston specialty coffee pop-up founded by Jasmine Rhodes. We serve lattes, espresso drinks and cold brew at celebrations, workplace events, community gatherings and brand collaborations.

What Types of Events Do You Serve?

Private celebrations (birthdays, showers, engagement parties, milestones), workplace events, community gatherings (markets, fundraisers, church and school events) and brand collaborations.

Where Do You Serve?

We serve Houston, TX. Include your venue or city in the inquiry form and we'll confirm whether we can be there.

What Drinks Do You Offer?

Specialty lattes including honey vanilla and brown sugar, classic espresso drinks and cold brew. Lattes can be served hot or iced.

How Do I Book a Coffee Pop-Up?

Fill out the inquiry form with your name, contact details, event date, estimated guest count, location and event type. We'll follow up to talk through your event and what we can serve.

What Should I Include in My Inquiry?

Your event date, estimated guest count, venue or city, event type, preferred contact method and any drink ideas or timing details.

How Much Does a Coffee Pop-Up Cost?

It depends on your event. Send your date, guest count and location through the inquiry form and we'll follow up to talk through details.

Do You Sell Merch?

VG shirts are coming soon. Join the list on the merch page to be notified when they drop.

Can My Brand Partner With Visionary Grinders?

Yes. We work with brands on launches, retail pop-ups and partner activations. Choose Brand Collaboration in the inquiry form.

Still Have Questions?

Send us your event details and we'll follow up.
